Thesis enrollment

Each semester the office releases a list of available thesis advisors in late March and early October. To keep the right to defend, enroll in THESIS before the add/drop deadline in the semester you plan to graduate.

Undergraduate prerequisites

Economics, Business Management, Statistics, and Accounting are prerequisite areas. Students from non-business majors must show an official transcript for at least one passed prerequisite before the first semester, or complete a suitable undergraduate course at FJCU in semester one.

Confirm course name and credits with the imMBA office in advance. Prerequisites do not count toward graduation credits.

Postgraduate waivers

Request professional-course waivers before official enrollment. There is only one chance, in the first week of class, subject to the chair’s approval.
