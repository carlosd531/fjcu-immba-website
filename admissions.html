Fu Jen imMBA is AACSB accredited. All courses are conducted in English. We offer two types of degree options:

  1. Two-year FJU imMBA program;
  2. Dual Master's degree programs: M1 at FJCU and M2 go abroad. Choose the University of Michigan–Flint (UM-Flint, USA), Kedge Business School (Kedge, France), or IQS School of Management (IQS, Spain). Students need to be admitted and pay extra full or partial tuition and other fees at partner schools. Students can obtain two master's degrees if the graduation requirements of two institutions are met.

Admissions calendar

We provide SPRING and FALL semesters for new students to enroll. SPRING application period: approximately mid-October to late December each year. FALL application period: approximately January to June each year.

This track is open to international students only. If you are not an international student, please refer to the Chinese version for admission information.

Tuition fees and scholarships

The standard tuition and miscellaneous fees for international students are NT$123,000 per semester. International students applying to the imMBA program may also apply for or qualify for the following:

  1. MOE Taiwan Scholarship
  2. MOFA Taiwan Scholarship
  3. FJCU Scholarship for New International Students in Master's Degree Programs

For recipients of scholarships 1–3, imMBA provides an additional tuition reduction from the international rate to the local rate: NT$123,000 → NT$68,660 per semester. For MOE and MOFA recipients, the local-student rate applies as long as the government scholarship remains active, for up to two years. For FJCU Scholarship recipients, the local-student rate applies for up to four consecutive semesters.

FJCU President's Scholarship. New imMBA international students from an English-speaking country or with TOEFL iBT 72, IELTS 6, or TOEIC 785 (CEFR B2 or above) receive a tuition reduction of NT$40,600 per semester for up to four consecutive semesters (NT$123,000 → NT$82,400). No separate application is required. A leave of absence results in loss of the scholarship. Students may receive only one of the four scholarships.

Course waiver (postgraduate level)

Students who have completed professional courses in the imMBA program and wish to apply for a course waiver must submit their request prior to officially enrolling in the program. There is only one chance to request, and the deadline is in the first week of class. Approval rests with the imMBA chair. More details. Undergraduate prerequisites are listed on Curriculum and Requirements.
