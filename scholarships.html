International tuition is NT$123,000 per semester. Choose either a local-rate reduction or the President’s Scholarship—not both. Confirm current rules in the admission brochure.

Government and FJCU new-student awards

Recipients of the MOE Taiwan Scholarship, MOFA Taiwan Scholarship, or the FJCU Scholarship for New International Students in Master’s Programs may pay the local-equivalent rate of NT$68,660 per semester while the award remains active.

Outbound awards

The Chinese official site also lists 學海飛颺 and 學海惜珠 awards for study abroad. Ask the office which cycle is open.

President’s Scholarship

New international imMBA students from English-speaking countries, or with TOEFL iBT 72, IELTS 6, or TOEIC 785 (CEFR B2, within two years), receive a NT$40,600 reduction per semester for up to four consecutive semesters—NT$82,400 payable. No separate application; it appears on the tuition slip.
