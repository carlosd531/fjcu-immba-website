The Chinese official site publishes the program meeting bylaw, curriculum committee bylaw, and student scholarship bylaw. This page summarizes what they cover; the official files remain the authority.

Program meeting bylaw

Sets how the imMBA program meeting is organized, who attends, and how decisions on teaching and student affairs are recorded.

Curriculum committee bylaw

Describes how the curriculum committee reviews course structures, new electives, and requirement changes before they are published.

Student scholarship bylaw

Explains internal awards administered by the program, including eligibility and the effect of a leave of absence.
