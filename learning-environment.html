About

Study in English, with the world in the room.

imMBA is designed for international students who want a professional classroom, a diverse cohort, and a campus in the Taipei metropolitan area.

English, every course

Teaching, discussion, and assessment are in English. The classroom is built for students who will work across markets, languages, and cultures.

A 1:1 international mix

Degree-seeking and exchange students come from more than 30 countries. Local and non-local students often sit side by side in equal numbers.

Taipei, close at hand

The program is based at Fu Jen’s Xinzhuang campus in New Taipei City, with direct access to Taipei’s companies, culture, and transport.

What the classroom feels like

Courses connect marketing, operations, finance, people, technology, and strategy. Recent electives include AI-assisted digital product development and data analytics for international business.

Outside class, students use two self-directed learning weeks each semester for industry visits, projects, or professional development—and an outdoor English corner that brings internationalization onto campus.

The College of Management is a four-Palme Eduniversal business school. That ranking reflects a long commitment to international collaboration, not a slogan on a brochure.

Support for international students

  • Program office in LM208-1, with English-speaking staff
  • Guidance on visas, housing, and campus life via the imMBA Guide
  • Tuition-free exchange if you do not take a dual degree
  • Dual-master’s options in the United States, France, and Spain
