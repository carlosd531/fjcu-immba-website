Degree options

  1. Two-year FJCU imMBA.
  2. Dual master’s: M1 at FJCU, M2 at UM-Flint, KEDGE, or IQS. Extra full or partial tuition may apply at the partner school. Both graduation requirements must be met.

Intake calendar

Spring: about mid-October to late December.
Fall: about January to June.

Required documents

  1. Autobiography (English)
  2. Study plan (English)
  3. English proficiency certificate (TOEFL, IELTS, or TOEIC). CEFR B2 preferred
  4. Two recommendation letters
  5. Any other supporting materials

The brochure may also list degree certificates, transcripts, a financial statement, nationality verification, and an application-fee receipt. Follow the current cycle instructions in the application system.

Tuition & scholarships

International tuition and miscellaneous fees: NT$123,000 per semester. Choose either preferential fees or the President’s Scholarship—not both.

Preferential fees
Holders of government scholarships or FJCU scholarships for new international master’s students may pay local-equivalent fees of NT$68,660 per semester.

President’s Scholarship
TOEFL iBT 72, IELTS 6, or TOEIC 785 (CEFR B2, within two years), or nationals of English-speaking countries, receive a NT$40,600 reduction per semester for up to four semesters (payable NT$82,400).

Course waiver

Postgraduate-level waivers must be requested before official enrollment, and only once, in the first week of class. Approval rests with the imMBA chair. See Courses for undergraduate prerequisites.
