Before you arrive

  • Accept your offer and complete registration steps in the university system.
  • Apply for a resident visa / ARC through the process advised by the Office of International Education.
  • Arrange housing: on-campus dormitories or nearby rentals in Xinzhuang and Taipei.
  • Budget for tuition, National Health Insurance after enrollment, and living costs in New Taipei / Taipei.

On campus

  • imMBA office: LM208-1, College of Management.
  • Courses are taught in English; campus services may be bilingual.
  • Fu Jen’s 16+2 calendar includes two self-directed learning weeks each semester.
  • Use the College of Management career platform for internships and jobs.
