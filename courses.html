Course waiver (postgraduate level)

Students who have completed professional courses in the imMBA program and wish to apply for a course waiver must submit their request prior to officially enrolling in the program. There is only one chance to request, and the deadline is in the first week of class. A course waived or not is subject to the imMBA chair's approval. More details.

Course waiver (undergraduate level)

Economics, Business Management, Statistics, and Accounting are imMBA prerequisite courses. Students with non-business-related majors are required to submit an official transcript to prove one of the prerequisites. Students failing to prove the above need to take and pass one prerequisite course at any FJCU undergraduate studies in the first semester at imMBA. It is mandatory to contact the imMBA office in advance to make sure the name and credits of the chosen prerequisite course are suitable and counted. imMBA graduation credits do not include prerequisite credits.

Thesis enrollment

Each semester, the imMBA office will release a list of professors in late March and early October for enrolled students to select their thesis advisor. Students are advised to wait patiently for the list. To preserve the right to apply for the thesis defense and graduate, students need to enroll in “THESIS” before the add/drop period in the same semester that they (may) plan to do so.
