Fu Jen Catholic University maintains a wide partner network with a tuition-free exchange program. imMBA students who are not on a contracted dual-degree path are encouraged to use it for a semester abroad and a broader cross-cultural experience.

Application procedures, deadlines, language requirements, and school lists are managed by the university’s international office—not by a separate imMBA form.

Visit the international office

Dual degree or exchange?

Dual degree: a structured 1+1 with UM-Flint, KEDGE, or IQS, extra partner tuition in some cases, and two diplomas.

Exchange: stay enrolled at Fu Jen, pay Fu Jen tuition, study abroad for a shorter period without a second degree.
