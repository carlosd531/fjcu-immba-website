Study schedule

Phase 1 · Fall

Submit the dual-degree application in week one. Follow the contract course plan. Confirm a thesis advisor and begin the thesis.

Phase 2 · Spring

Complete the first three-chapter thesis presentation. Provide English-proficiency proof and other contract documents. Finalize the thesis and defense agreement in final-exam week. Optional imMBA scholarship application.

Phase 3 · Fall

Register and pay full FJCU tuition. Take courses (and pay partner tuition where required). Send the registered course list to FJCU at the start of the semester.

Phase 4 · Spring

Continue registration at both schools. Complete partner requirements and imMBA graduation requirements, then defend at FJCU.

If qualified applicants exceed contract seats, the chair recommends students by junior standing, academic performance, and English proficiency. Partners make the final admission decision. Declare interest in week one of semester one so your Fu Jen course plan can be adjusted.

United States

UM-Flint

School of Management, University of Michigan-Flint. AACSB accredited; MBA with multiple concentrations. Typical year-one bar: thesis and agreement courses, GPA 3.0, TOEFL iBT as published by the partner. In-state tuition and scholarships may apply.

France

KEDGE

Triple crown (AACSB, AMBA, EQUIS). Master in Management (60 ECTS), plus at least six months of internship or practical experience. Bordeaux or Marseille. Year one: 240 ECTS from undergraduate study, thesis and agreement courses, English as published. Students pay full FJCU tuition; study at KEDGE is tuition-free under the agreement.

Spain

IQS Barcelona

AACSB; FT-recognized Master in Management. Choose MIMDE (International Marketing in a Digital Environment) or MIBM (Industrial Business Management). GPA and English as published. Tuition is reviewed annually; dual-degree students may receive a discount.
