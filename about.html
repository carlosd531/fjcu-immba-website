Overview and Ranking

Practical management, global mobility.

Fu Jen MBA in International Management is AACSB accredited and designed to give students usable business knowledge from a global perspective.

Only about 5% of business schools worldwide hold AACSB accreditation. The curriculum connects marketing, operations, finance, human resources, technology, and strategy so graduates can work in a current global economy.

Ranking

According to Eduniversal 2025, Fu Jen imMBA ranks top 3 in Taiwan and top 20 in Far East Asia in International Management. The College of Management has also been awarded 4 Palmes of Excellence in the Eduniversal Business Schools Ranking.

International classroom

The program recruits local and international students from more than 30 countries for exchange or degree study. In class, the ratio of local to non-local students can be 1:1—an environment built for cross-cultural work, not just international branding.

Two degree paths

  1. A two-year Fu Jen imMBA.
  2. A dual master’s: year one at FJCU, year two at UM-Flint, KEDGE, or IQS.

Students who do not take a dual degree can still join Fu Jen’s tuition-free global exchange.
